All 210 listed exhibitors, sorted into 18 security categories. Tap any exhibitor to see what it sells, its Singapore public-sector track record and its certifications, including CSA STAR. Star the stands you want, then group your shortlist by booth row to plan your walk.

Exhibitor names and booth numbers come from the official GovWare 2026 Sponsors & Exhibitors page as of 12 Oct 2026. The organiser updates the list periodically, so confirm booths onsite.

The categories are my own grouping. Exhibitors' self-selected tags on the official site are too broad to plan with (many small firms tick 15+ boxes), so each vendor is placed by what it actually sells: its GovWare profile for smaller and pavilion exhibitors, and its known product lines for established vendors. Platform vendors appear in several categories.

How the Singapore track record was checked (12 Oct 2026). Gov work means a public record of a contract, deployment or formal partnership with a Singapore agency: a GeBIZ award to the company, its product named in an agency's tender bought through a reseller, a GovTech panel seat, or a signed agreement with CSA or another agency. SG-recognised means a Singapore credential without a confirmed contract: a CSA licence for penetration testing or managed SOC, Singapore Common Criteria (SCCS) or IMDA MTCS certification, IMDA accreditation, or a CSA programme role such as SG Cyber Safe Advocate, the CyberSG startup zone or a GovTech STACKx speaking slot. None found means nothing turned up in those sources. That does not mean no work: global vendors usually sell to agencies through integrators, and tender descriptions rarely name the product.

Sources checked: CSA's licensed service providers list (9 Oct 2026), the SCCS product list, IMDA's MTCS register, CSA's SG Cyber Safe Advocates, GeBIZ award data via SGPBusiness (a third-party mirror; a S$1 award is a panel seat), agency and vendor announcements. CSA STAR is the Cloud Security Alliance's assurance registry for cloud services: Level 1 is a published self-assessment (CAIQ), Level 2 is an independent third-party audit, either STAR Certification (ISO 27001 plus the Cloud Controls Matrix) or STAR Attestation (SOC 2 plus the Cloud Controls Matrix). Each listing was checked in the STAR registry on 13 Oct 2026, by name search and the registry's usual page addresses. The registry has no reliable search, so a vendor marked as not listed may still have an entry under another company name; ask the vendor for its registry link. Levels often apply to specific cloud services, not the whole company, and STAR is irrelevant for hardware and pure services firms. International certifications list FedRAMP (US federal cloud authorisation) where confirmed for sponsors and CNAPP, identity and other key vendors; other exhibitors were not checked for it. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are near-universal for these vendors, so they are left out.
